Raise the stones before dawn.

    How to play
    • Move with the arrow keys or WASD (on a phone: the pad, or swipe on the board). Walk into a stone to push it. Holding a key keeps walking but never pushes, so a stone only moves on a fresh press. Click or tap a tile to walk there; tap a stone right next to you to push it.
    • Stones are tall blocks. Pushed upright, they topple onto the next two tiles. Pushed along their length, they rise on the tile beyond. Pushed from the side, they roll.
    • A moving stone knocks on any stone in its way, in the same direction, like dominoes. If anything in the chain is blocked, nothing moves.
    • Frost is slick: a pushed stone keeps tipping the same way while any part of it is on frost. A sliding stone that hits another stone knocks it on and stops; the knocked stone slides on if it lands on frost. You walk on frost normally.
    • Stand a stone on every socket. Plain stones count either way up. Marked stones (gold band) only count with the sun mark facing the sky; the band shows which end carries it.
    • The islands have walled shores: stones stop against the wall. Open water swallows a stone for good. Bog swallows a stone and turns to firm ground where it sank. You can't wade into either.
    • Dashed outlines (eye button or P) show where every push you can make right now would leave the stones, chains and slides included. If a push leaves the island unwinnable, a bar says so and Rewind takes you back. Hint (H) shows the next push, but a hinted solve earns no golden sun. Undo is free and unlimited.
