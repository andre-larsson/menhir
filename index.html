Islands

    How to play
    Goal
    Stand a stone on every socket. Marked stones (gold band) must stand with the sun to the sky.
    Move
    Arrow keys or WASD, or click a tile.Swipe the board, use the pad, or tap a tile.
    Push
    Walk into a stone, or tap it from beside it. Holding a key walks but never pushes.
    Stones
    A standing stone topples. A lying stone rises when pushed along its length and rolls when pushed from the side.
    Dominoes
    A moving stone knocks on any stone in its way. If anything in the chain is blocked, nothing moves.
    Frost
    A stone keeps tipping until it is off the ice. If it hits a stone, it stops and knocks that one on.
    Bog, water
    Bog swallows a stone and becomes firm ground. Water swallows it for good.
    Help
    Dashed outlines preview each push. Rewind undoes back to the last winnable position. Hint shows the next push, but costs the golden sun.
    Par
    The fewest pushes that solve the island. Match it without hints for a golden sun.
